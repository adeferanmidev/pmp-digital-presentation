About the project

Photographing Monumental People explores the human stories behind achievement, influence, faith, creativity and service. Through portraits and thoughtful conversations, it seeks to preserve perspectives and lessons that extend beyond a person's public identity.

This page is a shareable introduction to the project. The full editorial platform is available through the website.
